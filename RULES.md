## Tracks

Pick one track and build something that fits it.

1. **Kill the Busywork** — Take an annoying, repetitive job off someone's hands. Pick a real workflow and complete it, rather than just telling the user what to do. *Think: a tool that turns scattered event registrations into a usable check-in system, or a scanner that handles stock-taking for a small shop.*
2. **Make the Invisible Visible** — Help people notice what they normally miss, and do something useful with that information. *Think: software that reveals where a team's work keeps getting stuck, or a sensor that spots a water leak before someone notices the damage. The interesting part is what happens after detection.*
3. **Works Anyway** — Build for the conditions most products ignore: unreliable internet, an old phone, a language barrier, or someone who can't use a conventional interface. Choose one constraint, make your product work despite it, and demo it with the constraint actually present. *Think: an offline coordination tool for field teams, or a tactile controller that makes an everyday device easier to use.*
4. **Make Room for Play** — Build something people would want to experience, not just get work done with. Create a new way to play, make music, tell stories, or interact with other people. *Think: a game played across everyone's phones in the room, an interactive story whose world responds to players, or a musical instrument controlled through movement.*

## Participation and building

1. **Participate solo.** Teams are not allowed at this event.
2. **Start from scratch at the event.** Libraries, APIs, and public datasets are welcome. Pre-built projects are not allowed. This repository supplies documentation scaffolding only.
3. **Use Devin as you build, and briefly document your AI usage.** Keep a short, accurate record in [AI_USAGE.md](AI_USAGE.md).
4. **Submit your code repository, a short project description, and a working demo.** Use [SUBMISSION.md](SUBMISSION.md) to prepare these items. Follow the organizers' submission instructions.
