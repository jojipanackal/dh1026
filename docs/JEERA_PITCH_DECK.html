Jeera · Execution agent

The work between the tasks is where projects slip.

Jeera turns everyday team conversations into a living execution record: who is doing what, who is waiting, what changed, and when to check again.

A natural-language task runner for teams that need more than a board.

The coordination loop

Jeera turns silence into a useful question.

Morning

“What are my tasks for today?” The Agent gives a brief and asks what comes first.

During the day

“I am waiting on George.” The Agent records the dependency and its owner.

When work changes

“I got occupied.” Context is remembered without inventing a new status.

When evidence goes stale

The worker checks in with the assignee and includes the relevant task context.

At handoff

“It is ready.” The Agent captures what was verified and who can move next.

At close

Done includes completion details and an auditable history, not just a checkbox.
